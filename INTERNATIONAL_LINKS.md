# International links on the Nigeria website

The Nigeria website owns local information: national leadership, branch locations, local events, news, Nigerian media channels, and national contact details.

Some links intentionally open the International Offices website instead of duplicating the material locally:

- **Giving** uses the established church-wide giving destination and its maintained payment process.
- **Ministry resources** point to the departments that maintain official descriptions, logos, themes, and approved materials.
- **Doctrine and approved resources** may have a Nigerian introduction, while the International Offices page remains the source for the complete approved wording.
- **Branding and church-wide policies** remain with the International Offices because official marks and permissions are administered centrally.

This approach avoids stale or conflicting copies. The website should create a local page whenever the information, service, contact point, or legal obligation is specific to Nigeria. External links should be clearly labelled and should open in a new tab where that helps visitors understand that they are leaving the Nigeria website.
